# Artist Distribution Agreement

**Effective {{ version }}** · DRAFT, pending attorney review

This agreement is between you (the "Artist," which also covers the band, label or company you represent) and {{ entity }} ("Suprm Sounds"). It applies to every recording, artwork file and piece of metadata you upload (your "Content"). It's part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Rights you give us
For as long as your Content is on the Service, you grant Suprm Sounds a **non-exclusive**, worldwide license (unless you limit the territories) to:
- reproduce, encode and deliver your Content to Stores and to our distribution partners;
- let Stores sell, stream and make your Content available, and promote it using clips and artwork;
- collect the revenue Stores report for your Content on your behalf;
- use your name, likeness, artwork and approved biography to promote your releases and the Service.

**You keep ownership of your masters and your songs.** We never own your music. Because the license is non-exclusive, you can distribute the same recordings elsewhere, but don't deliver the same release to the same Store through two distributors. Stores treat that as a duplicate and may take both down.

## 2. Your promises
You promise that:
- you own or control all rights in the sound recordings and artwork, or have written licenses for them. That includes samples, interpolations, beats (with a lease or license that allows commercial distribution) and featured performances;
- for cover songs, you'll get any mechanical licenses the law requires where Stores don't handle them;
- your metadata (titles, artist names, credits, songwriter names, explicit flag, release date) is accurate and doesn't impersonate anyone;
- the splits you enter are correct and agreed with every payee;
- your Content doesn't break any law or anyone's rights.

## 3. Money
- **What we collect:** the net amount Stores and our distribution partners actually pay us for your Content, after their fees ("Net Receipts").
- **Our share:** our commission, if any, as shown in your account at the time the earnings are reported. The rest is credited to you and your split payees.
- **Statements:** your earnings dashboard shows every line reported to us, by Store, country, month and track.
- **Payouts:** you can be paid once your balance reaches the minimum payout and you've connected a verified payout account. We pay only from money we've actually received from Stores.
- **Taxes:** you're responsible for your own taxes. Our payment processor collects tax forms (W-9/W-8) and issues tax documents where required. We may withhold amounts the law requires.
- **Corrections:** if a Store reverses, corrects or claws back earnings, or we credit you by mistake, we may deduct the amount from your future earnings.

## 4. Splits and collaborators
You can direct shares of a track's earnings to collaborators by email. Their shares build up for them even before they create an account. Split changes apply only to earnings reported after the change. If collaborators dispute a split, we may hold the disputed earnings until they agree in writing or a court decides.

## 5. Withholding for fraud and claims
We may hold, reverse or deduct earnings, and take releases down, if:
- a Store flags artificial streams or other fraud on your Content (see the [Content & Fraud Policy](/legal/content-policy)); any fees or penalties a Store charges us because of your Content can be deducted from your earnings;
- someone makes a credible rights claim against your Content, until it's resolved;
- you break this agreement.

## 6. Takedowns
You can ask us to remove a release at any time. We'll send takedowns to Stores within 5 business days, but each Store removes it on its own timeline. Earnings reported for streams and sales before the takedown are still paid out.

## 7. Term
This agreement lasts until you close your account or remove all your Content, or until we terminate it under the Terms of Service. Sections 2, 3 (for earnings already reported), 5 and 8 continue after it ends.

## 8. Indemnity
You'll defend and compensate Suprm Sounds, its partners and the Stores against claims, losses and costs (including reasonable legal fees) that arise from your Content, your metadata, your splits or your breach of this agreement.

## 9. General
If there's a conflict between this agreement and the Terms of Service about distribution or money, this agreement wins. Everything else in the Terms of Service applies, including governing law and disputes.
