# Content & Fraud Policy

**Effective {{ version }}** · DRAFT, pending attorney review

Stores hold distributors responsible for everything we deliver. This policy keeps your music on the Stores and keeps everyone's payouts safe.

## Artificial streaming is banned
Don't use, buy or encourage:
- bots, click farms, "stream-for-stream" groups, looped playback devices, or paid playlist placement that guarantees streams;
- promotion services that promise a number of streams or followers.

Stores detect this and **penalize the distributor, and we pass those penalties on to the account responsible.** If a Store flags your release, we may:
1. hold the earnings on the affected tracks while we investigate;
2. deduct any fees or penalties the Store charges us;
3. take the release down;
4. close repeat or serious offenders' accounts and keep the fraudulent earnings.

Legit promotion is fine: ads, playlist pitching that doesn't guarantee plays, social content and press.

## Rights and samples
- Upload only music you own or have licensed. Uncleared samples, and beats downloaded without a license that allows commercial distribution, are the most common reason releases get taken down.
- Remixes, edits and mashups need permission from the owners of the original recording and song.
- Cover songs are allowed. Credit the original songwriters and don't imitate the original artwork or the original artist's name.

## AI-generated and AI-assisted music
- You must hold the rights to every part of your release, including anything an AI tool generated. Check the tool's license terms.
- Don't use AI to imitate a real artist's voice or style in a way that could make listeners think they performed it.
- Disclose AI use when we or a Store asks. Stores' rules on AI content change often, and we follow the strictest rule that applies to your release.

## Metadata rules
- Use the same artist names consistently, and never use another artist's name to get attention.
- Put featured artists in the credits, not in the track title.
- No generic titles meant to game search (e.g. "Relaxing Sleep Music 1–500"), no mass uploads of near-duplicate tracks, and no misleading artwork.

## What we'll reject
Hate speech, content that sexualizes minors, content that promotes violence or terrorism, and anything else a Store or the law prohibits.
