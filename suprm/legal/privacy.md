# Privacy Policy

**Effective {{ version }}** · DRAFT, pending attorney review

This policy explains what {{ entity }} ("Suprm Sounds") collects, why we collect it and what choices you have. Questions: {{ email }}.

## What we collect
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, artist name, email, country, password (stored only as a salted hash) | To run your account and contact you |
| Legal name | Credits, rights paperwork and payouts |
| Music, artwork, metadata, credits and splits | To deliver your releases and pay the right people |
| Earnings data reported by Stores | To show your earnings and calculate payouts |
| Collaborator emails you enter for splits | To credit their share and invite them |
| Basic usage and device data (IP address, browser, pages visited) | Security, fraud prevention and fixing bugs |

**Payout details go to Stripe, not to us.** Your bank account, tax ID and identity documents are collected and stored by our payment processor, Stripe, under [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). We only see whether your account can receive payouts.

## Who we share it with
- **Stores and distribution partners:** your Content and release metadata, which is how distribution works.
- **Service providers:** hosting, file storage, email delivery and payments (Stripe). They may use it only to provide their service to us.
- **Collaborators:** people you add to a split see your artist name and the release it belongs to.
- **The law:** when a valid legal process requires it, or to protect rights, safety or prevent fraud.

**We don't sell your personal information.**

## How long we keep it
While your account is open. After you close it, we keep financial and tax records for as long as the law requires (generally 7 years) and delete or anonymize the rest within 90 days.

## Your choices and rights
You can see and update your details in your account and download your earnings history. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or send you a copy of it, by emailing {{ email }}. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU/UK), you may have more rights under the CCPA or GDPR, and we'll honor them.

## Security
We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, private file storage with short-lived access links, and two-factor login for staff accounts. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## Children
The Service isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We'll post updates here and email you about material changes.
